Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

What we collect

  • Scanned URLs. URLs you submit are processed to produce the safety report. Scan results are stored so that reports can be shared via permalink and, for signed-in users, shown in scan history.
  • Account data. If you create an account: your email address and a salted, hashed password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Operational data. Standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) kept briefly for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We don't set tracking or advertising cookies. The only cookie we use is a session cookie for signing in.
  • We don't build behavioral profiles of visitors.

Third-party lookups

To analyze a link, our servers may query public registry (RDAP) services and, when configured, threat-intelligence services such as Google Safe Browsing. Only the URL or domain being scanned is shared with those services — never your identity.

Payments

There aren't any. Linkaware is free, we don't process payments, and we never collect card or billing information.

Your rights

You can clear your scan history from the dashboard at any time. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at privacy@Linkaware.app.

Changes

We'll update this page when the policy changes and revise the date above.
