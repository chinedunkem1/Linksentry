      How it works

      Three layers of analysis, one clear verdict.

      1

      Structural analysis

      We dissect the URL itself: look-alike characters, embedded credentials, deceptive subdomains, high-abuse domain extensions, and the wording tricks phishers rely on.

      2

      Live inspection

      Our servers safely follow every redirect the link takes — shorteners included — to reveal the true final destination and flag encryption downgrades along the way.

      3

      Domain intelligence

      We check public registry records for the domain's age. Scam sites live on domains registered days ago; established businesses don't.

      Built for real-world threats

      Every check maps to a technique attackers actually use.

      🎣 Phishing detection

      Brand impersonation, credential-bait keywords, and look-alike punycode domains are flagged before you ever load the page.

      🔗 Shortener unmasking

      bit.ly, tinyurl and 15+ other shorteners are followed to their true destination — you see where the link really goes.

      🕵️ Redirect tracing

      The full hop-by-hop redirect chain is displayed, exposing cross-domain forwarding and HTTPS downgrades.

      📅 Domain age lookup

      Live registry (RDAP) data reveals freshly registered domains — the strongest single indicator of a scam.

      🔒 Encryption checks

      TLS certificate validation, plain-HTTP pages, and encrypted-to-unencrypted downgrades are called out so your data never travels in the clear.

      🛡️ Threat database checks

      Frequently asked questions

      Is it safe to scan a suspicious link here?

      Yes. The link is never opened in your browser. Our servers fetch only the response headers needed to trace redirects — no page content executes on your device.

      Does a "Safe" verdict guarantee the site is legitimate?

      No scanner can promise that. A low risk score means none of our structural, network, or registry checks raised a flag. Always stay cautious with pages that ask for passwords or payment details.

      Do you store the links I scan?

      Scans are processed in memory and returned to you. We don't build profiles, set tracking cookies, or sell data.

      Can I integrate Linkaware into my own product?

      Yes — there's a free REST API. Create an account, generate a key in your dashboard, and see the API documentation to get started.
