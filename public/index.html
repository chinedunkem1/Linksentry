        How it works

        Three layers of analysis, one clear verdict.

        01

        Structural analysis

        We dissect the address itself: look-alike characters, embedded credentials, deceptive subdomains, high-abuse domain extensions, and the wording tricks phishers rely on.

        02

        Live inspection

        Our servers safely follow every redirect the link takes — shorteners included — to reveal the true destination, check its certificate, and grade how the site is run.

        03

        Threat intelligence

        We check the domain's age and hosting, then verify the link against Google Safe Browsing — the same threat database that protects Chrome.

        Built for real-world threats

        Every check maps to a technique attackers actually use.

        Phishing detection

        Brand impersonation, credential-bait keywords, typosquats and look-alike punycode domains are flagged before you ever load the page.

        Shortener unmasking

        bit.ly, tinyurl and 15+ other shorteners are followed to their true destination — you see where the link really goes.

        Redirect tracing

        The full hop-by-hop redirect chain is displayed, exposing cross-domain forwarding and encryption downgrades.

        Domain age lookup

        Live registry data reveals freshly registered domains — the strongest single indicator of a scam.

        Threat database checks

        Every scan is verified against Google Safe Browsing's live blocklist, the same data protecting Chrome.

        Visual preview

        See a screenshot of the destination rendered in a remote sandbox — look before you go, without the page touching your device.

        Hosting intelligence

        Who hosts the site, in which country, on what network — infrastructure tells a story scammers can't easily fake.

        Certificate checks

        Expired, self-signed and untrusted certificates are called out, along with plain-HTTP pages that expose what you type.

        Site fingerprint

        Server software, tech stack and a security-header grade reveal how professionally the destination is run.

        Frequently asked questions

        Is it safe to scan a suspicious link here?

        Yes. The link is never opened in your browser. Our servers fetch the page and follow redirects on your behalf — nothing from the site executes on your device.

        Does a clear result guarantee the site is legitimate?

        No scanner can promise that. A low risk score means none of our structural, network, or registry checks raised a flag. Always stay cautious with pages asking for passwords or payment details.

        Do you store the links I scan?

        Scan results are stored so reports can be shared by link and, if you have an account, listed in your history. We don't build profiles, set tracking cookies, or sell data.

        Can I use this in my own product?

        Yes — there's a free REST API. Create an account, generate a key in your dashboard, and see the API documentation to get started.
